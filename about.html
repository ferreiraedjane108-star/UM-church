History of UM Church

UM Church started in 1998 with 12 people in a living room in Sea Point. Vision: Hope & Faith to Cape Town.

1998 - 12 members in Sea Point
2005 - Woodstock, 150 members
2015 - 123 Faith Road, 400+ members
2026 - 700 members, 3 services, Youth projects

Our Mission: Hope for the broken, Faith for the future, Love for Cape Town.
